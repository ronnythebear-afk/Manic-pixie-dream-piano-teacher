There's a ghost in the piano.

It's trying to write a love song, but it can't find the words by itself. It can only hear them.

Play softly and it comes out. Each key shakes loose a different kind of word. Catch the ones you want, then press Sing, and the ghost sings your line with the notes the words came from.

Play too fast and it hides.

Keys A–K play. Space catches. Enter sings.
